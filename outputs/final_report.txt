======================================================================
 C.  BEHAVIORAL EQUIVALENCE
======================================================================

We feed the projected token sequences back in as ACTUAL TEXT and compare
the model's answer to (a) gold, (b) CODI's own greedy answer.

Two input formats:
  text           : [question, projected_tokens]  (no <|bocot|>/<|eocot|>)
  latent_via_text: [question, <|bocot|>, projected_tokens, <|eocot|>]
                   — matches CODI's training structure

Baselines (what happens WITHOUT soft prompts):

   CODI (real latent reasoning)              38.87   100.00
   [q, <|bocot|>, <|eocot|>]                       31.64   63.87   ← empty structural wrapper
   [q] (question only)                       25.78   39.06

>>> Conclusion C: behavioral equivalence FAILS across the board.
    Key findings:

    1. The empty wrapper [q, <|bocot|>, <|eocot|>] already recovers 63.9%
       of CODI's answers and 31.6% gold accuracy.  Most of CODI's behavior
       comes from question + structural markers, not from the specific
       content of the latent reasoning positions.

    2. Inserting the projected tokens between <|bocot|> and <|eocot|>
       performs WORSE than the empty wrapper (38% agree vs 64%). The
       projection is adversarial noise from the model's point of view.

    3. Inverse scaling in K: higher K means MUCH better reconstruction
       cosine (K=64 reaches 0.999) yet WORSE behavioral equivalence
       (K=16 gold 31%, K=64 gold 23%). More capacity lets the optimizer
       find solutions further off the token-reachable manifold.

    4. Without structural markers the projection is catastrophic: the
       K=32 'text' mode (just [q, projected]) gets only 6.8% gold.

======================================================================
 OVERALL INTERPRETATION
======================================================================

The user's diagnostic maps to: 'A high but C fails → the states are
text-reachable but nearest-token projection is lossy.'  We strongly
confirm this: reconstruction cosine saturates near 1.0, projected text is
pure noise, and behavior transfers poorly.

More specifically, the INVERSE SCALING in K (lower K gives better
behavior) is diagnostic: with few soft embeddings the optimizer is
squeezed closer to the token-reachable manifold and the projection is
less lossy. This is consistent with a language-model prior regularizer
being the right fix (user's suggestion at the end of the spec).

Additionally, the empty_latent baseline is a striking surprise: simply
tokenizing <|bocot|><|eocot|> around the question recovers 64% of CODI's
output behavior. This suggests CODI's latent reasoning contributes only
a modest ~7% absolute accuracy gain (31.6% → 38.9%) on top of the model's
zero-shot capability elicited by the trained structural markers. The
projection approach therefore has at most ~7 pts of 'content' to recover,
but loses most of it due to projection lossiness.
